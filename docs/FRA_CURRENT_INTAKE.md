# Current FRA intake and report (as published)

Source: https://developments.tradelineassociates.com/fra/ (captured 2026-10-07).
This is the existing paid Funding Readiness Audit service. The tool must produce this report.

## Intake questions (in order)

### 1. Business Owner
- Full Legal Name * (first, last)
- Phone Number *
- Email Address *
- Home Address (street, line 2, city, state, zip, country)
- What percentage of the business do you own? (20% or less / 50% or less / 80% or less / 100%)

### 2. Business Information
- Exact legal name of business * (as on formation and IRS records)
- DBA
- Business EIN
- Entity Type (C-Corp / S-Corp / Professional Corporation / Partnership / LLC / Sole Proprietor / Other)
- State originally formed
- Exact date business was originally formed
- Current primary business address
- Primary business phone
- Business email
- Description of products/services
- Business website (or "No Website")

### 3. Banking & Debt
- Primary business bank
- How long has this business bank account been open? (<3 mo / 3–6 mo / 6–12 mo / 1–2 yr / 2+ yr)
- Existing business debt (multi: No / Business credit cards / Business LOC / Term loan / SBA loan / Equipment financing / MCA / Revenue-based financing / Other)
- Approximate total business debt ($)
- Average 30-day business bank balance (or "I Don't Know")
- Existing business banking relationships (free text)
- Previous banking problems (Yes/No) + follow-up: institution, account type, date, reason, balance owed

### 4. Business Credit IDs ("I Don't Know" allowed)
- Primary NAICS code
- SIC code
- D-U-N-S number
- Experian Business Identification Number (BIN)
- Equifax Commercial Identification Number

### 5. Personal Credit
- How to provide 3-bureau FICO info: upload report / provide credit-monitoring access (service + username)
- Upload 3-bureau FICO report
- NAV account (Yes/No) + NAV username/password

### 6. Business Documents (uploads)
- EIN / SS-4
- Formation documents
- Business license
- Last 3 months business bank statements
- YTD P&L (if available)
- Balance sheet (if available)
- Most recent business tax return (if available)
- Any business credit reports

### 7. Review & checkout
- Certification: information accurate; audit is an assessment, not a guarantee of financing.

## Report structure promised to clients
1. Your first three actions (items that gate everything else)
2. Key funding strengths
3. Overall assessment: five lender areas rated (Legal & identity, Business credit, Banking, Guarantor, Funding goal)
4. 20-point business compliance audit, each point Pass / Needs Improvement / Action Required / Pending / N/A with a one-line finding
5. Bureaus, UCC & financials: Experian, D&B, Equifax business reviewed separately
6. Roadmap & action center: phased corrections, correction tracker, official channels to fix each item

Composite score is issued only when the file is complete enough ("We don't publish a composite score on an incomplete file").

### Part II: Funding Readiness Analysis
- Commercial credit bureaus (Experian, D&B, Equifax independently): scores, tradelines, payment experiences, identity fields
- Banking & financials: statements, balances, tax returns, financial statements, business debt
